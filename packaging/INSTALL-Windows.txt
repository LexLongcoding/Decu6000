DECU6000 - Windows (64-bit)
===========================

VST3 PLUGIN
  1. Copy the whole "Decu6000.vst3" folder to:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
     (Windows will ask for administrator permission - click Continue.)
  2. In your DAW, rescan plugins. Decu6000 appears as an instrument.

STANDALONE
  Double-click Decu6000.exe - no install needed.
  Click "Options" (top-left) -> "Audio/MIDI Settings..." to choose:
    - your audio output (pick "Windows Audio (Exclusive Mode)" for low latency), and
    - your MIDI input (tick your MPD18 / keyboard under "Active MIDI inputs").

  Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the app
  isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click "More info" ->
  "Run anyway". This only happens once.

PATCHES
  Your saved patches live in  %APPDATA%\Decu6000\Patches
  (paste that into the Explorer address bar). The Standalone and the VST3
  share the same patches.

MANUAL
  See Decu6000-User-Manual.pdf for every control, MIDI learn, patches and tips.
