DECU6000 - Linux (x86_64)
=========================

VST3 PLUGIN
  Copy the "Decu6000.vst3" folder to   ~/.vst3/
    mkdir -p ~/.vst3 && cp -r Decu6000.vst3 ~/.vst3/
  Then rescan plugins in your DAW (Bitwig, Reaper, Ardour, Carla...).

STANDALONE
  ./Decu6000          (if needed first:  chmod +x Decu6000)
  Click "Options" (top-left) -> "Audio/MIDI Settings..." to choose ALSA or
  JACK, your output device and your MIDI input (MPD18 / keyboard).
  - JACK / PipeWire-JACK: start your JACK server (or use pw-jack ./Decu6000).
  - ALSA: pick your card; if another app holds the device, use "default"
    or "pulse"/"pipewire" from the device list.

REQUIREMENTS
  Needs the usual desktop libraries: ALSA (libasound2), fontconfig, freetype.
  JACK is optional and loaded only if installed.

PATCHES
  Saved patches live in  ~/.config/Decu6000/Patches

MANUAL
  See Decu6000-User-Manual.pdf for every control, MIDI learn, patches and tips.
